    GOOD TO KNOW

    What Verisafe can and can’t tell you

    What it checks

    • The file name, the link, and common tricks such as fake brand sites.
    • If you choose a file, its first bytes and risky features in PDFs and scripts, within the first 5 MB.

    What it can’t tell you

    • Verisafe looks for warning signs. It cannot prove a file is safe.
    • A low risk result means nothing suspicious was found, not that the file is clean.
